structure can be built, i. e., wherein all of the uranium
bodies are of metallic uranium, but the combination of
metal and oxide in the present example is utilized for
economy.

"reactor" is preferred.

In any moderator, neutron bombardment during use
in a reactor tends to purify the moderator. For example,
boron, an element having a high capture cross section is
converted to lithium by neutron capture and subsequent
alpha emission, and lithium has a much smaller capture
cross section than boron. In D2O, light water contami
nation is reduced by conversion of the light water to

heavy water by neutron capture. Thus moderators im
prove with use, if not re-contaminated.
