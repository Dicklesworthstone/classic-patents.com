Sedimentation Test: To a solution of 1.0 g. of dry lithium

chloride in 30 ml. of dry N,N-dimethylacetamide is added 0.5
g. of dry polymer powder and comminuted to a particle size of 55
about 20 p. or less. The tube is stoppered and its contents,
heated at 60°~80° C., are subjected to stirring by a mechanical
agitator for a period of from 10 min. to 4—5 hrs. 1f polymer
particles remain visible, the contents of the tube are cooled to
—70° C. (e.g., by immersion in a bath of solid carbon dioxide 60
and acetone), then are allowed to warm up until stirring can
be resumed, and are heated as above. The tube is then allowed

to stand upright for a further 24 hours without stirring. After
this time, if no polymer residue lies settled on the bottom of
