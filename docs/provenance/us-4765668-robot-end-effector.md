## Editorial and preservation boundaries

- The pinned PDF above is the immutable facsimile.
- `public/patents/transcripts/us-4765668-robot-end-effector-reviewed.txt` is
  a page-marked, human-reviewed comparison ledger, not the public source face
  and not a raw OCR or PDF-text-layer export.
- `src/data/editions/robotEndEffectorEdition.ts` is the continuous
  visitor-facing archival edition. It contains every printed claim and omits
  scan-page banners.
- Figure previews under
  `public/patents/figures/us-4765668-robot-end-effector/` are source crops
  selected from the pinned drawing sheets; they are not reconstructed diagrams.
- The grant's preferred embodiment supplies a 5 mm ball-screw lead, 2,000 N
  maximum gripping force, 43 mm/s maximum travel, 0.05 mm repeatability, and
  a typical 6-inch jaw opening. A simulator may use these disclosed values but
  must not manufacture an unprinted payload, finger geometry, contact law,
  pressure-to-force transfer, or robot-arm kinematics.
