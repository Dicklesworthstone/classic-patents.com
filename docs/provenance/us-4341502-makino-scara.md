## Editorial and preservation boundaries

- The pinned PDF above is the immutable facsimile.
- `public/patents/transcripts/us-4341502-makino-scara-reviewed.txt` is a
  page-marked, human-reviewed comparison ledger; it is not the public source
  face and is not a raw OCR or PDF-text-layer export.
- `src/data/editions/makinoScaraEdition.ts` is the continuous visitor-facing
  archival edition. It contains every printed claim and intentionally omits
  scan-page banners.
- Figure previews under `public/patents/figures/us-4341502-makino-scara/`
  are unaltered source-sheet renderings selected from the pinned drawing
  sheets. They are not reconstructed diagrams.
- Editorial engineering text may explain planar closed-chain kinematics, but
  the grant supplies neither link lengths, motor torque, payload, stiffness,
  nor a numerical compliance law. Those quantities are not asserted as patent
  facts or emitted as live physical telemetry.
