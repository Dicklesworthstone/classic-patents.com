## Editorial and preservation boundaries

- The visitor-facing source face is
  `src/data/editions/lemelsonAdjustableManipulatorEdition.ts`. It is a
  continuous hand-authored reading, not a PDF text layer or scan-page display.
- `public/patents/transcripts/us-3260375-lemelson-adjustable-manipulator-reviewed.txt`
  is the page-marked research ledger used to compare the edition to this eleven-page
  facsimile. Its page markers are not shown in the public edition.
- Facsimile-derived drawing-sheet crops live below
  `public/patents/figures/us-3260375-lemelson-adjustable-manipulator/`. Every authored
  figure reference in the edition points to one or more of those local files.
- The grant prints seventeen numbered claims. The archival edition preserves all
  seventeen, and the catalogue record reads the exact claim text dynamically from that
  edition rather than duplicating legal text.
- Google Patents HTML may aid comparison only. Published source text, figure references,
  claims, and paragraph boundaries were checked against the pinned facsimile; no machine
  draft or text layer is presented as a reviewed edition.
- The grant supplies kinematic and sequencing topology: carriage travel, vertical
  translation, azimuth rotation, a pivot joint, jaws, adjustable stops, limit switches,
  and forward/stop/reverse control branches. It does not state dimensions, motor torque,
  speed, acceleration, payload, jaw force, positional accuracy, cycle time, or production
  rate. The interactive kernel therefore models normalized pose and source-described
  switch sequencing only, and explicitly refuses quantitative mechanical-performance
  claims.
