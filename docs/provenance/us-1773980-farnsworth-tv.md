## Editorial and preservation boundaries

The visitor-facing source face is the typed `farnsworthTvArchivalEdition` in
`src/data/editions/farnsworthTvEdition.ts`; it is continuous and does not
reproduce scan-page furniture. The reviewed page-marked ledger is
`public/patents/transcripts/us-1773980-farnsworth-tv-reviewed.txt`. Direct
source sheet crops for each printed figure reference are under
`public/patents/figures/us-1773980-farnsworth-tv/`. The grant prints eighteen
claims. The older `public/patents/source-text/us-1773980-farnsworth-tv.txt`
is a machine text layer only: it is research evidence, never the published
edition or reviewed ledger.

The local PDF is the correct US 1,773,980 source, but it is not the later
single-tube/magnetic-raster account that the former record mixed into this
catalogue entry. Its stated system uses a photo-electric plate, fixed
electrical shutter, differently timed electrical potentials, radio link,
light rotator, and quartz oscillographs.
