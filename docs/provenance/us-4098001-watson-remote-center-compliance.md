- `src/data/editions/watsonRccEdition.ts` is the visitor-facing manual source
  face. Its typed blocks are reviewed against this facsimile; it does not
  present a PDF text layer or machine OCR as historical text.
- The reviewed, page-marked comparison ledger will be
  `public/patents/transcripts/us-4098001-watson-remote-center-compliance-reviewed.txt`.
  It is evidence for the manual edition, not the public source face.
- Figure previews will be source crops from these three drawing sheets under
  `public/patents/figures/us-4098001-watson-remote-center-compliance/`. They
  are facsimile citations, not reconstructed diagrams.
- The grant prints two claims. Both must remain separate claim blocks and the
  canonical data record must dynamically read their literal text from the
  manual edition.
- The specification supplies topology and qualitative behavior, not calibrated
  geometry, elastic constants, loads, or cycle times. Editorial visualizations
  must expose that boundary rather than presenting inferred values as patent
  facts.
