## Identity and correction record

The primary source identifies the grant as *Endless Conveyer or Elevator*, US
470,918, to Jesse W. Reno of New York, N. Y. It gives application serial
**376,455**, not 376,515. It has **three** printed claims, each drafted as a
separate combination claim; the record therefore presents all three as
independent rather than manufacturing dependencies.

The previous catalogue source was not a transcription of this grant. It
introduced an unsupported citizen/preamble sentence, an unsupported freight
and station description, a fabricated three-part “invention consists” list,
and different claim text. It also supplied unprinted dimensions, machine
components, performance figures, safety claims, and historical assertions.
The manual edition retains only assertions supported by the facsimile or
states the source boundary explicitly. In particular, the grant describes
cast-iron belt sections, not a specified wood-cleat chain; gives 200 feet per
minute and 6,000 single-file passengers per hour; and says only that the
apparatus is automatic, not that it meets any modern safety standard.
