## No-formal-claims finding

All twelve source pages were inspected a second time for a claim heading,
numbered claim, claim sentence, or other formal claim clause. None appears.
The document contains a detailed schedule/description and execution material;
it does not contain a numbered claims section. The record must therefore state
an evidence-backed no-formal-claims condition and must not substitute a modern
summary of the mechanism for historical claim text.
