## Prior ledger correction history — 2026-08-21

Direct cloud/PDF review confirmed that the grant ends at Claim 25, while the former ledger rewrote claims into
shorter, materially different language. Its former Claim 25 described hermetic
packaging; the printed Claim 25 limits Claim 24 to a P-N junction capacitor.
The former drawing-sheet entries also invented Figures 9–20. The replacement
edition preserves the full specification sequence and exact claim literals.
This correction history is retained as audit evidence; it does not suppress the
visitor's complete patent-text access.
