## Root publication hold — 2026-08-21

The canonical record remains visitor-withheld. Direct cloud/PDF review confirms
that the grant ends at Claim 25, while the former ledger rewrote claims into
shorter, materially different language. Its former Claim 25 described hermetic
packaging; the printed Claim 25 limits Claim 24 to a P-N junction capacitor.
The former drawing-sheet entries also invented Figures 9–20. The replacement
edition now preserves the full specification sequence and exact claim literals
as a candidate, but it is not attached to the record until the page-marked
ledger, every semantic figure reference, and source-approved crops receive an
independent facsimile acceptance pass.
